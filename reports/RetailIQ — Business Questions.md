# RetailIQ — Business Questions

## Objective
The objective is to convert the cleaned e-commerce datasets into actionable business insights through exploratory data analysis, financial analysis, visualization and predictive modelling.

## Business Questions
1. Which products and categories generate the most revenue and profit?
2. Which countries, regions and sales channels perform best?
3. Which customers contribute the most revenue?
4. Which customers have not purchased recently?
5. Are discounts associated with lower profit margins?
6. Which delivery methods have more delays?
7. Which customer segments have higher return rates?
8. Can we predict future monthly sales?
9. Can we identify customers at risk of becoming inactive?
Product & Sales Performance
10. Which products have high sales but low profit margins?
11. Which products have experienced the highest growth or decline in sales over time?
12. Which product categories contribute the largest share of total revenue?
13. Which products are frequently purchased together in the same order?
14. How do sales vary by month, quarter, season, or day of the week?
Customer Behaviour & Marketing
15. Which customer acquisition channels bring in the most valuable customers?
16. What percentage of customers make repeat purchases, and how does it vary by segment?
17. What is the average time between consecutive purchases for repeat customers?
18. Which customer segments have the highest average order value?
19. Which marketing campaigns generate the highest revenue and profit?
20. Are coupon users more likely to make repeat purchases than non-coupon users?
Operations & Financial Performance
21. Which shipping methods have the highest average shipping costs?
22. What percentage of orders are delivered later than the estimated delivery date?
23. How do discounts, taxes, and shipping costs affect final profit margins?
24. Which regions have the highest cancellation rates, and how do they affect revenue?

## Planned Deliverables
- Validated financial metrics and reconciliation report
- Product, category, geography and channel performance analysis
- Customer revenue and recency analysis
- Discount and profit-margin analysis
- Delivery delay and return-rate analysis
- Monthly sales forecasting model
- Customer inactivity-risk model
- Interactive Power BI dashboard

## Validation Principles
- Avoid double-counting order-level and item-level sales.
- Validate revenue, discounts, costs and profit before analysis.
- Define return, cancellation and inactivity metrics consistently.
- Document assumptions, missing values and limitations.
- Evaluate predictive models on data not used for training.
